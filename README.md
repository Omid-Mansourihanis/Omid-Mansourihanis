# Hi, I'm Omid Mansourihanis 👋

### PhD Candidate | Geospatial Machine Learning Researcher | Urban Data Scientist

I develop innovative machine learning and geospatial analytics frameworks to address urban equity, spatial justice, and sustainable development challenges across major U.S. cities.

I am currently pursuing my PhD in Geosciences at **Texas Tech University**, where I conduct research at the intersection of geospatial science, urban planning, machine learning, remote sensing, and public policy.

My work focuses on uncovering hidden patterns in complex urban data and developing data-driven solutions for more accessible, sustainable, and equitable cities.

## 🚀 Current Research

My current research investigates how geospatial machine learning, satellite observations, network analysis, and urban socioeconomic data can be combined to:

- Identify unequal environmental and transportation burdens
- Measure urban accessibility and spatial disadvantage
- Analyze land-use compatibility and urban form
- Explain the drivers of traffic congestion
- Develop equity-sensitive transportation and routing models
- Predict the impacts of urban policies across neighborhoods
