I develop machine learning and geospatial analytics frameworks to address urban equity, spatial justice, and sustainable development challenges across major U.S. cities.

I am a PhD candidate in Geosciences at the **Center for Geospatial Technology, Texas Tech University**, working at the intersection of geospatial science, urban planning, machine learning, remote sensing, and public policy. My work focuses on uncovering patterns in complex urban data and turning them into evidence for more accessible, sustainable, and equitable cities.

## 🚀 Current Research

I combine geospatial machine learning, satellite observations, network analysis, and socioeconomic data to:

- Identify unequal environmental and transportation burdens
- Measure urban accessibility and spatial disadvantage
- Analyze land-use compatibility and urban form
- Explain the drivers of traffic congestion
- Develop equity-sensitive transportation and routing models
- Predict the neighborhood-level impacts of urban policies
